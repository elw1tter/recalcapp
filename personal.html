Who is Evan Witter outside of academics?

Background

My family is originally from the Indiapolis area, but moved to the Denver area just over 2 years ago, so proximity to big cities is part of what drew me to Boston College. When I am back at home, I enjoy mountain biking, hiking, skiing, and playing piano. In Boston I spend a lot of my free time exploring the city and looking for new plaecs to eat.

The origin of my Finance interest

When I was young, my dad started a small industrial internet of things company. When I was in high school, I watched close-by as this company neared financial distress, dealing with issues such as revenue growth and unsustainable spending. Seeing this close up introduced me to the depth and complexity of finance, and my desire to explore this is what initially led me to where I am today.

Respite Care Provider (Summer 2025)

I worked with families to provide respite care to special needs individuals. This experience was a genuine introduction to the dynamic of working in a fast pace, team based enviornment, something I sought for more of once arriving on campus as a freshman.

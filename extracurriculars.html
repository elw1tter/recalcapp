Extracurricular envolvement has created depth in my interest in finance

BC Investment Club General Member Stock Pitch Competition

I pitched Generac, a back-up generator manufacturer with important residential and commercial/industrial applications. I argued that weather based cyclicality were overshadowing a trend of rising grid infrastructure and instability, undervaluing a real structural demand driver.

Won first out of over 150 competitors, becoming an analyst as a result. This was my first genuine hands on finance experience, and the ability to apply an analytical mindset and build out a model and thesis further sparked my interest in investment banking.

BC Investment Club Analyst - Industrials Group

Working in a group of analysts, I have gained more experience applying an analytic mindset in a team environment. Consistent involvement has allowed to me to further develop my skills, and gotten me more of the experience I previously enjoyed with my pitch competition, as I am currently working on a team to build a buy pitch for Hubbell.

BC Credit & Restructuring Club

Involvement in BCCRX has allowed me to explore my general finance interest in a variety of applications. By attending weekly lectures, following case studies, and participating in a credit pitch competition, I have discovered an interest in an area I previously knew little about.

Pitched a short sell on a Lumen Parent Company senior unsecured note, arguing that a previous divesture that used proceeds for a substantial refinancing materially worsened this specific note's position, while improving the companies debt profile as a whole on paper.
